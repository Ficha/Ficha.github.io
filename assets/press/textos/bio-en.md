# Fidel Chaves bios (English)

## One line
Fidel Chaves (Buenos Aires, 1996) is a biologist, writer and editor in training.

## Short (~50 words)

He published the short story collection *Cinco fragmentaciones del alma* (El Buscador, 2018) and has written the newsletter *Diario de un Robot* since 2020.

## Long (~150 words)
Fidel Chaves was born in Buenos Aires in 1996. He holds a degree and a teaching qualification in Biological Sciences from the University of Buenos Aires, where he now studies Editing. He took part in research on bee behavior published in *Scientific Reports* and, since 2023, works as a science communication specialist at a biotechnology company. He writes in Spanish, English and French.

In 2018 he published the short story collection *Cinco fragmentaciones del alma* (Editorial El Buscador) and is currently editing *La chispa*, his second collection. He keeps two newsletters: *Diario de un Robot* (since 2020), fiction and essays on science, technology and what makes us human, and *A quien corresponda* (since 2022), on literature and writing prompts. He also runs creative writing workshops for teenagers and adults.
